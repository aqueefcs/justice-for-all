# Compliance checklist

Each item needs an owner and has to be signed off by the legal team before the phase it gates.

## Before MVP (content only)
- [ ] "Legal information, not legal advice" disclaimer: wording approved, and shown on every content screen
- [ ] Terms of Use and Privacy Policy (English + every supported language)
- [ ] Bare acts sourced from India Code (reproduction allowed, Copyright Act s.52(1)(q)); source and date shown
- [ ] Content review workflow: every published item has a named lawyer reviewer
- [ ] Grievance officer named in the app (IT Rules 2021)

## Before accounts (Phase 2)
- [ ] DPDP Act 2023 / DPDP Rules 2025:
  - [ ] consent notice per purpose, in the user's language
  - [ ] consent log
  - [ ] withdrawal of consent
  - [ ] export and deletion of data
- [ ] Data retention policy for documents and accounts
- [ ] Breach response plan (who, what, notification timelines)
- [ ] Lawyer verification process (enrolment number, State Bar Council certificate)

## Before paid consultations (Phase 3)
- [ ] **Written legal opinion on the fee model and listing design under Bar Council of India Rule 36** (no advertising or solicitation)
- [ ] Neutral lawyer listing: no rankings, ratings or "top" labels
- [ ] Fee split shown clearly (lawyer fee vs platform fee); refund policy
- [ ] Razorpay KYC and GST registration as applicable
- [ ] Consultation recording policy: default off, and only with explicit consent from both sides

## Governance
- A judge has no role in the software. A retired judge may serve on the offline Legal Advisory Board.
