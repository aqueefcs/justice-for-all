# Justice For All brand: "Open Law"

The mark combines three ideas:
- **An open book:** the law, opened up and made readable for everyone.
- **A balance rising from its spine:** fairness and justice.
- **A point of light above:** hope, and the person who is seeking help.

The dot and stem also form a lowercase **"i"**, the international sign for *information*. This is what the app offers: legal information.

## Type
- Headings and wordmark: **Space Grotesk**
- Body text: **Inter**

Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font License, which permits their use in logos and apps.

## Originality and usage
- The mark was drawn from scratch using basic geometric shapes. No stock art, templates or existing logos were used.
- The balance is a generic public-domain symbol of justice. The combination of book, balance and light/"i" is original to this project.
- It deliberately does **not** use the State Emblem of India or the Ashoka Chakra, because using those is restricted by law.
- Before registering the mark as a trademark, run a search on the IP India trademark registry (classes 9, 42 and 45).

Do not recolour the mark outside the brand colours, stretch it, or add effects. Keep clear space around it at least the size of the dot.
